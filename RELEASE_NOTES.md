### XennMap v1.0.0

**Your Offline Sea Map Companion.**

An offline-first marine map app for Filipino fishermen, boat operators and coastal
travelers. Download your home waters once — the chart, depth bands, saved spots and
navigation assistance work with zero signal.

### Included

* Offline-first map experience (MapLibre + OpenStreetMap-based tiles)
* Nationwide depth-band grid with contours (clearly labeled chart data)
* GPS position, heading and live chart depth under the boat
* Save any location — from GPS or by tapping the map anywhere
* Categories, favorites, search and navigation assistance
* Track recording with breadcrumb trails
* Notify list on the Dashboard
* Offline area downloads: coastal presets, custom frame-on-map areas
* Dark and Light themes
* Private by design — nothing leaves your device

### Installation

1. Download `app-release.apk` below.
2. On your Android device (8.0+), open the APK and allow installing from that
   source if asked.
3. Grant location permission and download your home waters over Wi-Fi before
   sailing.

### Important

XennMap is a navigation-assistance application. It does **not** replace official
nautical charts, professional marine navigation equipment or safety practices.
The depth layer shows chart data — never a live sonar measurement.
